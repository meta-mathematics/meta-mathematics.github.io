\section{Target and import contract}

\begin{theorem}

For a \(C^3\) unit-speed curve \(\gamma:I\to\mathbb R^3\) with curvature
\(\kappa=\|\gamma''\|>0\), define \(T=\gamma'\), \(N=T'/\kappa\),
\(B=T\times N\), \(\tau=-B'\cdot N\). Prove \(T'=\kappa N\),
\(N'=-\kappa T+\tau B\), and \(B'=-\tau N\).

\end{theorem}

\subsection{Foundations and permitted imports}

Use a \(C^{3}\) unit-speed curve \(\gamma\) with
\(\kappa =\Vert \gamma ''\Vert >0\). Differentiation of inner products
and cross products is foundational. T,N,B form the positively oriented
frame with \(B=T\times N\); torsion is defined as \(\tau =-B'\cdot N\).
No Frenet--Serret formulas are imported.

\section{Worked orchestration}

For the helix \(\gamma (s)=(a\) \(\operatorname{cos} (s/c),a\)
\(\operatorname{sin} (s/c),b\) s/c), where \(a>0,c=\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}}\),
speed is 1. Calculation yields \(\kappa =a/c^{2}\) and
\(\tau =b/c^{2}\). For \(b=0\) the circle has \(\tau =0\) and the
binormal is constant.

\subsection{Boundary and hypothesis audit}

A straight unit-speed line has \(\kappa =0\). It has many possible
normal directions, so this Frenet frame is undefined there. Sense blocks
division by \(\kappa\); an alternative frame such as a parallel/Bishop
frame would be a separately specified construction.
