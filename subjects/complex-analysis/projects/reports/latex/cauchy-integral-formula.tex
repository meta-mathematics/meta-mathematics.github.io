\noindent\textbf{CA-01 / Complex Analysis / Report v1}

\section{Target and import contract}

\begin{theorem}

Let \(f\) be holomorphic on an open neighborhood of the closed disk
\(\overline{D(z_0,R)}\). For \(w\) inside the disk, prove
\(f(w)=\frac1{2\pi i}\oint_{|z-z_0|=R}f(z)/(z-w)\,dz\), with positive
orientation.

\end{theorem}

\subsection{Foundations and permitted imports}

Holomorphic means complex differentiable on an open neighborhood of the
closed disk. Import Cauchy--Goursat in a finitely connected region with
piecewise smooth boundary: the positively oriented boundary integral of
a holomorphic function is zero. Also use continuity and the
parametrization of a circle. Inner boundary components carry clockwise
induced orientation.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Numbered mathematical activities}

\begin{enumerate}[label=N2.\arabic*,leftmargin=3.4em]

\item Split \(f(z)=f(w)+(f(z)-f(w))\).

\item The constant part integrates to \(2\pi if(w)\), since
\(z=w+\rho e^{it}\) gives \(dz/(z-w)=i\) dt.

\item The remainder satisfies \(|\oint (f(z)-f(w))/(z-w)\) \(dz|\le 2\pi\)
\(\operatorname{sup} _|z-w|=\rho |f(z)-f(w)|\to 0\) by continuity.

\item The outer integral is independent of \(\rho\), so taking \(\rho \to 0\)
gives \(\oint \operatorname{outer}\) \(f(z)/(z-w)dz=2\pi if(w)\), the
desired formula.

\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked orchestration}

For \(f(z)=z^{2}+1\), \(z_{0}=0,R=2,w=1\), the formula predicts
\(\oint (z^{2}+1)/(z-1)dz=4\pi i\). Algebraically
\(z^{2}+1=(z-1)(z+1)+2\); the polynomial term integrates to 0 and
\(2/(z-1)\) integrates to \(4\pi i\).

\subsection{Boundary and hypothesis audit}

For w on the boundary the integrand is singular on the contour, and the
excision argument is unavailable. Sense rejects the interior formula
there; boundary limiting theorems require a different contract.
Reversing the contour gives the negative of the stated integral.
